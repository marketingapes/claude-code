3M Military Earplug Lawsuit

Active Mass Tort Case · $6B+ Settlement Fund

Did you serve in the U.S. military between 2003–2015?

3M supplied defective Combat Arms Earplugs (CAEv2) to every branch of the military. The earplugs were too short to properly seal the ear canal, allowing damaging noise levels through. 3M knew about the design flaw and sold them anyway.

You may qualify if:

  • You served in any U.S. military branch between 2003–2015
  • You were issued or used Combat Arms Earplugs
  • You suffer from hearing loss, tinnitus (ringing in ears), or both
  • You have a VA disability rating for hearing-related conditions

This was the largest mass tort in U.S. history with over 300,000 claims. 3M agreed to pay over $6 billion in settlements. Time to file is limited.
