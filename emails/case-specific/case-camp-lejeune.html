Camp Lejeune Water Contamination

Active Mass Tort Case · New Federal Law Enables Claims

Were you or a family member stationed at Camp Lejeune between 1953–1987?

For over 30 years, service members and their families at Marine Corps Base Camp Lejeune in North Carolina were exposed to drinking water contaminated with toxic chemicals — including known carcinogens like TCE, PCE, benzene, and vinyl chloride.

The PACT Act (2022) now allows veterans and family members to file claims against the federal government for harm caused by Camp Lejeune water contamination — even if prior claims were denied.

You may qualify if:

  • You lived or worked at Camp Lejeune for 30+ days between 1953–1987
  • You were diagnosed with cancer, Parkinson's, liver disease, kidney disease, or other serious conditions
  • A family member stationed there developed health issues
  • A family member who was stationed there has passed away from related illness

Estimated settlement range: $25,000 – $500,000+ depending on diagnosis and exposure duration.
