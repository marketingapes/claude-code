Talcum Powder Cancer Lawsuit

Active Mass Tort Case · J&J Set Aside $8.9B for Claims

Did you regularly use talcum powder or baby powder?

For decades, Johnson & Johnson sold talc-based baby powder while internal documents showed they knew it contained asbestos — a known carcinogen. Tens of thousands of women have filed claims linking talcum powder to ovarian cancer and mesothelioma.

You may qualify if:

  • You regularly used talcum/baby powder in the genital area
  • You were diagnosed with ovarian cancer
  • You were diagnosed with mesothelioma
  • You used brands like Johnson's Baby Powder, Shower to Shower, or other talc products

Individual jury verdicts have exceeded $4 billion. J&J has reserved $8.9 billion specifically for talc settlement payouts.
