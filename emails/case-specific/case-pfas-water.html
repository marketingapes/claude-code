Has your community's water been contaminated with PFAS?

PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances) — called "forever chemicals" because they never break down — have been detected in the drinking water of over 2,500 communities across the United States.

The EPA now states there is NO safe level of PFAS exposure. These chemicals are linked to cancer, thyroid disease, immune disorders, reproductive issues, and liver damage.

Companies like 3M, DuPont, and Chemours manufactured and discharged PFAS for decades while concealing the health risks. Major settlements are now being reached.

You may qualify if:

  • You live near a military base, airport, or industrial site that used PFAS-containing foam
  • Your local water system tested positive for PFAS contamination
  • You were diagnosed with kidney or testicular cancer
  • You developed thyroid disease, ulcerative colitis, or immune disorders
  • You experienced pregnancy complications or fertility issues
