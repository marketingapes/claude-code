From Ocean Waste to Everyday Wonder

Every year, 8 million tons of plastic enter our oceans. We intercept that waste before it reaches the sea and transform it into beautiful, functional products you'll use every day.

Our supply chain is fully transparent. We partner with coastal communities in Southeast Asia, providing fair-wage employment while cleaning up beaches and waterways.

Designed to Disappear

When our products reach the end of their long life, they don't sit in a landfill for centuries. Our packaging is 100% compostable. Our products are recyclable or biodegradable.

We believe the best products leave no trace — except the positive impact they create along the way.
