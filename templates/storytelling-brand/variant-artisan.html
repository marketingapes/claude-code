Our Story
The Beginning

It Started in a Garage

In 2018, founder Maria Chen left her corporate design job with a simple conviction: people deserve products made with care. Armed with her grandmother's leather-working tools and a relentless attention to detail, she began crafting bags from a 200-square-foot garage.

Word spread. Each bag told a story — of late nights perfecting stitches, of sourcing leather from family-owned tanneries in Tuscany, of refusing to cut corners even when no one would notice.

Today

Still Handmade. Still Honest.

Today, our team of 12 artisans works from a sunlit studio in Portland. We've grown, but our values haven't changed. Every product is still cut, stitched, and finished by hand. We know our makers by name, and we know their families.

"The moment you hold something truly handmade, you feel the difference. That feeling is what we live for."

— Maria Chen, Founder

Our Process

1. Source

Premium full-grain leather from three family-owned tanneries we've partnered with since day one.

2. Cut

Each piece hand-cut by our master craftspeople. No two hides are alike, and we embrace that.

3. Stitch

Saddle-stitched by hand with waxed linen thread. Stronger than machine stitching, with character only hands can give.

4. Finish

Edges burnished, hardware fitted, final inspection. Every piece must pass Maria's quality check before shipping.
